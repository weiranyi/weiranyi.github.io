AI双语译文（Bilingual Translation）

Microchip implants, mind‑control and cybernetics

微芯片植入物、精神控制与控制论

Author: Rauni‑Leena Luukkanen‑Kilde, specialist physician, Norway

作者：劳尼‑莱娜・卢卡宁‑基尔德，挪威专科医师

In 1948 Norbert Wiener published the book Cybernetics, which dealt with a comprehensive neurological theory of control and communication, already circulating in small circles at that time.

1948 年，诺伯特・维纳出版了《控制论》，该书阐述一套完整的神经层面控制与通信理论，当时这套理论已经在小圈子中流传。

Yoneji Masuda, the father of the information‑society concept, voiced his concern in 1980 that our freedoms are being threatened in the style of an Orwellian society for a large part of humanity.

信息社会概念之父增田米二于 1980 年表达担忧：我们的自由正以奥威尔式社会的形态，威胁着绝大多数人类。

The very first brain implants were inserted in 1946, without the knowledge of the subjects’ parents.

最早的脑部植入物于 1946 年被植入人体，受试者的父母对此毫不知情。

Devices began to be placed in animals and humans for the study of bodily functions and the brain.

人们开始把装置植入动物与人体内，用于研究身体机能和大脑。

The so‑called mind‑control methods aimed to turn human behaviour and attitudes into tools, especially for intelligence services.

所谓的精神控制手段旨在把人的行为与态度变成工具，主要供情报机构使用。

Thirty years ago, brain implants were visible in X‑ray images. Today they have become tiny.

三十年前，脑部植入物还能在 X 光影像中被看见。如今它们已经变得极其微小。

The future goal is to inject a microchip into every newborn to function as an identifier.

未来的目标是给每一名新生儿注射微芯片，用作身份识别装置。

Likewise in Sweden, where Olof Palme in 1973 authorised implantations for prisoners, and where Jan Freesen, Director‑General of the Data Inspection Board, exposed this technology.

瑞典同样如此：奥洛夫・帕尔梅在 1973 年批准对囚犯实施植入手术，瑞典数据监察局局长扬・弗瑞森揭露了这项技术。

These human test subjects can be tracked anywhere, and their brain activity can be influenced by remote‑controlled supercomputers by altering frequencies.

这些人类试验对象可以在任意地点被追踪；超级计算机可以通过改变频率进行远程操控，以此影响他们的大脑活动。

Prisoners, war veterans, homosexual people, single women and other groups are used as guinea‑pigs.

囚犯、战争幸存者、同性恋者、单身女性以及其他人群被当作试验小白鼠。

For instance, the experiences that have come to public light from inmates in a US Utah state prison are shocking.

例如美国犹他州一所监狱囚犯公之于众的亲身经历令人触目惊心。

A microchip operates by means of low‑frequency radio waves directed at it.

微芯片依靠定向作用于它的低频无线电波工作。

Via satellites, a person carrying an implant can be precisely located.

借助卫星，携带植入物的人可以被精确定位。

Dr Carl Sanders reported that this technology was used, for example, during the Iraq War.

卡尔・桑德斯博士称，该技术例如曾在伊拉克战争中投入使用。

He invented the IMI, Intelligence Manned Interface biochip, which is injected into human beings.

他发明了 IMI，即智能人机接口生物芯片，可被注射进人体内。

During the Vietnam War, soldiers were injected with the so‑called “Rambo chip”, which boosted adrenaline secretion into the bloodstream.

越南战争期间士兵被注射所谓 “兰博芯片”，该芯片会提升血液中的肾上腺素分泌水平。

At the same time, NSA computers programmed for 20 billion bits per second were capable, via the RMS Remote Monitoring System, of “seeing and hearing” what soldiers experienced on the battlefield.

与此同时，美国国家安全局每秒处理 200 亿比特的计算机，通过 RMS 远程监控系统，能够 “看见并听见” 士兵在战场上的亲身感受。

Today the microchip is only 30 micrometres in diameter (a human hair is 50 micrometres thick).

如今这种微芯片直径仅有 30 微米（一根头发丝为 50 微米）。

It can record experiences, smells, sights and sounds.

它可以记录人的感受、气味、视觉景象与声音。

A person linked to the computer can relive those experiences later on.

接入计算机的人之后可以重新体验这些记录下来的内容。

The RMS system can send electromagnetic messages as signals via the hypothalamus to implanted individuals, affecting human performance.

远程监控系统可以经由下丘脑，向植入芯片的个体发送电磁信号信息，影响人的行为表现。

Using microwaves, healthy people can be made to experience hallucinations or auditory phenomena.

借助微波，可以让健康人产生幻觉或者幻听。

Every thought, reaction, hearing and visual observation produces certain potentials (spikes, patterns) in the brain.

每一个想法、反应、听觉、视觉感知都会在大脑产生特定电位（尖峰信号、信号模式）。

Electromagnetic fields (EMF) can be decoded into thoughts, images and sounds.

电磁场（EMF）能够把这些信号解码为思想、图像与声音。

EMF brain‑stimulation can alter human brain‑waves and affect, among other things, muscle motor function, causing painful muscle cramps and similar symptoms.

电磁场脑部刺激可以改变人的脑电波，还会影响肌肉运动功能，造成剧烈肌肉痉挛以及其他类似症状。

The NSA’s electronic surveillance system can simultaneously monitor and interrogate millions of people.

美国国家安全局的电子监控系统可以同时监控、审问数百万人。

Each person has their own unique interrogation parameters.

每个人都有一套专属的审问参数。

Through EMF brain‑stimulation, fully coded pulsing electromagnetic signals can be transmitted into the brain, producing desired auditory and visual effects in the target person.

通过电磁场脑部刺激，可以向大脑传输完整编码后的脉冲电磁信号，在目标者身上制造出预期的听觉、视觉效果。

This technology is applied, for instance, in electronic warfare.

该技术例如被应用于电子战。

Astronauts received implants before being sent into space.

宇航员在送往太空之前就被植入了芯片。

All their bodily functions could be monitored from the ground, and all their thoughts and emotions were recorded around the clock.

地面端可以监控他们全部身体机能，他们所有想法与情绪都被全天候记录。

The Washington Post reported in May 1995 that after the abduction threat against Britain’s Prince William, human microchip‑implant technology emerged.

《华盛顿邮报》1995 年 5 月报道：在英国威廉王子遭遇绑架威胁后，人体微芯片植入技术问世。

After implantation, a signal is sent via satellite to computer terminals at police headquarters, where the kidnapped person’s movements can be followed and their position determined.

植入完成后，芯片发出信号，经由卫星传送到警方总部计算机终端，可以追踪被绑架者的行动并定位。

The press has not reported that human privacy is lost for good.

媒体并未报道人的隐私就此永久丧失。

A human being can be manipulated in any way whatsoever.

人可以被以任意方式操纵。

By using different frequencies, human emotional states can be altered.

通过使用不同频率，人的情绪状态可以被改变。

A target can be made aggressive or lethargic; their sexuality can be influenced, their thought‑signals can be read, their sleep can be disturbed.

可以使人变得攻击性强或者萎靡倦怠；可以干预其性取向、读取思维信号、干扰睡眠。

Even their own unknown subconscious mind with its motives can be uncovered without their knowledge.

甚至可以在当事人毫不知情的情况下，挖掘出其本人都不了解的潜意识与内在动机。

The perfect cyber‑soldier has been created!

完美的赛博士兵就此被造出来！

This technology has been in use in some NATO countries since the early 1980s, without civilian‑academic circles learning about it in professional publications.

这项技术自二十世纪 80 年代初就已经在部分北约国家投入使用，民间学术界却没有从专业出版物得知此事。

NSA Signals Intelligence can remotely monitor information from human brains by decoding evoked potentials of 3‑50 Hz, 5‑millivolt electrical discharges.

美国国家安全局信号情报部门可以远程监测人脑信息，解码 3‑50 赫兹、5 毫伏的诱发电位。

Identical brain injuries have been observed among elderly test subjects both in Gothenburg and Vienna in 1998.

1998 年，哥德堡与维也纳两地，充当试验对象的老年人身上都发现了相同类型的脑损伤。

Disturbed blood circulation and oxygen shortage in the right frontal lobe occur.

会出现右额叶血液循环紊乱、脑部缺氧。

Case records show brain atrophy and loss‑of‑consciousness seizures caused by oxygen deprivation.

病例记录显示有脑萎缩，以及缺氧引发的昏厥发作。

The purpose of present‑day mind‑control systems is to bring a target person or group under complete external control, so that they act against their own convictions and interests.

当代精神控制系统的目的，是让目标个人或群体完全处于外部控制之下，使其做出违背自身信念与利益的行为。

They can be programmed, for example, to commit murder.

可以对他们进行 “编程”，例如驱使他们犯下谋杀。

The technology is used for political purposes.

该技术被用于政治目的。

Since 1980, the ESB system (Electrical Stimulation of the Brain) has been actively used.

自 1980 年起，ESB 脑电刺激系统被实际投入使用。

It is used to control various selected target persons without their knowledge.

它被用来在当事人不知情的情况下控制各类选定的目标人物。

All international human‑rights conventions prohibit such operations, even inside prisons — not to mention civilian populations.

全部国际人权公约都禁止这类操作，哪怕在监狱内也不允许，更不用说针对普通平民。

The US Senate discussed the problem of civilian‑population irradiation on the basis of a proposal by John Glenn, most recently on 22 January 1997.

美国参议院依据约翰・格伦的提案，于 1997 年 1 月 22 日专门讨论过针对平民群体的定向辐射问题。

This includes directing electromagnetic radiation at human brain activity.

其中就包含将电磁辐射对准人脑活动。

Besides electronic mind‑control techniques, chemical methods are also in use.

除电子精神控制技术之外，化学手段同样在被使用。

“The Silent War” also covers NSA “kit‑techniques” used by military and intelligence services.

“无声战争” 也包含美国国家安全局供军方、情报机构使用的整套工具技术。

These include mind‑altering drugs, various brain‑function‑suppressing gaseous substances sprayed into ventilation ducts or water‑supply networks.

包括改变心智的药物、多种抑制大脑功能的气态物质，它们被喷洒进通风管道或是供水管网。

There is documented evidence that bacteria and viruses have also been used for this purpose in different countries.

有档案证据表明，细菌与病毒同样在多国被用于该目的。

According to its most advanced achievements, today’s cutting‑edge technology — connecting our brain‑activity via microchips, or even without chips, through satellites to supercomputers in the United States and Israel — represents the greatest threat to humanity and privacy.

按照其所谓的最高技术成果，当今尖端技术：借助微芯片（甚至无需芯片），经由卫星将人脑活动接入美国、以色列的超级计算机，是对人类与隐私的最大威胁。

The newest supercomputers can process communications from the entire planet.

最新一代超级计算机可以处理来自整个地球的信息通信。

This is the greatest threat in human history.

这是人类历史上最严重的威胁。

Once people are lured into receiving microchips in their bodies under false pretences, or through mandatory legislation such as has been planned in the USA, robotisation of humanity and total disappearance of private life together with thought‑control will become reality.

一旦人们被花言巧语欺骗植入微芯片，或是依靠强制立法（美国已有相关计划），人类机械化、私人生活连同思想管控彻底消亡就会成为现实。

How many of us would want to hand over our whole lives, including our innermost thoughts, to an outside controller who certainly does not have our best interests in mind?

我们当中有多少人愿意把自己全部人生，包括内心最隐秘的想法，交给一个绝不会为我们利益着想的外部控制者？

A totalitarian “New World Order” with its neurological communication systems already exists to stop people’s independent thinking, social and political action.

一套带有神经通信系统的极权 “新世界秩序” 已然存在，目的是阻止人类独立思考、开展社会与政治行动。

Once our brain‑activity is connected to supercomputers via radio implants and microchips, it will be too late to wake up.

一旦我们的大脑活动通过无线电植入物、微芯片连上超级计算机，那时再醒悟就为时已晚。

The threat can only be countered by exposing this matter right now, studying relevant literature and information obtained at international congresses.

只有立刻揭露这件事、研读相关文献以及国际会议得到的资料，才能够对抗这项威胁。

An interesting detail about this technology is that its cover‑up has worked extremely well.

关于这项技术有一个耐人寻味的细节：对它的掩盖工作做得极为成功。

For example, the development of the psychiatric diagnostic manual DSM in 18 languages by psychiatrists from US intelligence services for cover‑up purposes.

例如美国情报机构精神科医师为掩盖真相，参与编写、翻译成 18 种语言的精神疾病诊断手册 DSM。

Victims are systematically diagnosed as mentally ill according to symptom descriptions we learn already during our studies.

受害者会系统性地被按照教科书上学到的症状描述，诊断成精神病患者。

We are never taught that patients may be telling the truth when they become unwilling guinea‑pigs for military electronic, chemical and bacteriological warfare.

从来没有人教过我们：有些患者说的可能是真话 —— 他们被迫沦为军方电子、化学、细菌战的试验对象。

Time is running out if we wish to reverse the direction of military‑medical research, not to speak of humanity’s future.

如果我们想要扭转军事医学研究的走向，更不必说守护人类的未来，留给我们的时间已经不多。
