Microchip Implants, Mind Control and Cybernetics

微芯片植入物、精神控制与控制论

Rauni-Leena Luukanen-Kilde — Specialist Physician, Norway

Rauni-Leena Luukanen-Kilde——专科医生，挪威

In 1948, Norbert Weiner published the book Cybernetixs, which referred to a complete neurological theory of control and communication that was already in use within a small circle at that time. In 1980, Yoneji Masuda, the father of the concept of the information society, expressed his concern that our freedom was threatened, in the manner of an Orwellian society, by this technology, which was completely unknown to most people. It connects people through microchips to a brain–satellite–computer link.

1948年，诺伯特·维纳出版了《控制论》一书，书中提出了一套完整的神经控制与通信理论，当时该理论已在一个小范围内使用。1980年，“信息社会”概念之父增田米治表示担忧：这种大多数人不了解的技术，正以奥威尔式社会的方式威胁着我们的自由。它通过微芯片，将人接入一个“大脑—卫星—计算机”连接系统。

The first brain implants were surgically inserted as early as 1874 in Ohio, USA, and at the Karolinska Institute, brain electrodes were implanted in children as early as 1946 without their parents’ knowledge.

早在1874年，美国俄亥俄州就通过手术植入了首批大脑植入物；早在1946年，卡罗林斯卡学院就在父母不知情的情况下，为儿童植入了大脑电极。

In the 1950s and 1960s, actual microchip implants began to be inserted into animals and humans for research purposes, especially in the USA, with the aim of studying human behaviour, bodily functions and the brain, among other things. The purpose of the so-called mind-control methods was to change human behaviour and attitudes. Influencing brain activity became an important military weapon, especially for the intelligence services.

20世纪50至60年代，人们开始出于研究目的，将真正的微芯片植入物植入动物和人体，尤其是在美国，其目的包括研究人的行为、身体机能和大脑。所谓精神控制方法的目的，是改变人的行为和态度。影响大脑活动由此成为一种重要的军事武器，尤其被情报机构所重视。

Thirty years ago, implants inserted into the brain appeared on X-rays as objects approximately one centimetre in size. Later, implants were reduced to the size of a grain of rice and made of silicon, and subsequently of gallium arsenide. Today, they have become small enough to be injected into the neck, the back, under the skin or into various parts of the body during operations. It is now almost impossible to remove them. The intention is that, in the future, a microchip will be injected into every newborn child and will then serve, among other things, as identification for the rest of that person’s life. In the USA, legislative plans for this are already well advanced. The same applies in Sweden, where Olof Palme gave permission in 1973 for prisoners, for example, to be implanted, and where, according to Jan Freese, Director-General of the Data Inspection Board, nursing-home patients were implanted in the mid-1980s, among others. The Swedish government report SOU 1972:47 revealed the technology. These human guinea pigs can then be tracked anywhere, and their brain activity can be influenced by remote control from supercomputers by altering frequencies.

30年前，植入大脑的植入物在X光片上约有一厘米大小。后来，植入物缩小到米粒大小，先由硅制成，随后改用砷化镓。如今，它们已经小到可以在手术过程中注射到颈部、背部、皮下或身体其他部位，而且几乎无法取出。其意图是在未来向每个新生儿体内注射一枚微芯片，使其在余生中发挥身份识别等作用。在美国，这方面的立法计划已经取得很大进展。瑞典也是如此：奥洛夫·帕尔梅于1973年批准了对囚犯等人群进行植入；据瑞典数据监察局总干事扬·弗雷泽称，养老院患者等人群也在20世纪80年代中期被植入了芯片。瑞典政府报告SOU 1972:47披露了这项技术。此后，这些人类实验对象无论身处何地都能被追踪，超级计算机还可以通过改变频率，远程影响他们的大脑活动。

Prisoners, conscripts, mental-hospital patients, disabled children, deaf people, blind people, people with intellectual disabilities, homosexuals, lonely women and other groups of people are used as guinea pigs. For example, the publicly reported experiences of inmates at a prison in the US state of Utah are shocking.

囚犯、应征士兵、精神病院患者、残疾儿童、聋人、盲人、智力障碍者、同性恋者、孤独女性等人群被用作实验对象。例如，美国犹他州一所监狱囚犯公开披露的经历就令人震惊。

The microchip operates by means of a low-frequency radio wave directed at it. Satellites can be used to locate a person carrying an implant with precision. According to Dr Carl Sanders, this technology was used during the Iraq War, among other occasions. He invented the IMI, an “intelligence manned interface” biochip, which is injected into human beings. During the Vietnam War, soldiers were injected with the so-called Rambo chip, which increased the secretion of adrenaline into the blood.

微芯片通过指向它的低频无线电波工作。借助卫星，可以精确定位体内带有植入物的人。据卡尔·桑德斯博士称，这项技术曾在伊拉克战争等场合使用。他发明了IMI，即“智能载人接口”生物芯片，可将其注射到人体内。越南战争期间，士兵被注射了所谓的“兰博芯片”，以增加血液中的肾上腺素分泌。

At the same time, the National Security Agency’s computers, programmed for 20 billion bits per second, were able, through their Remote Monitoring System, RMS, to “see and hear” what soldiers experienced on the battlefield. When a microchip—now only 30 micrometres in diameter, compared with 50 micrometres for a human hair—is placed in the optic nerve of the eye, it absorbs people, experiences, smells, sights and sounds from the brain in the form of nerve impulses. Once transferred to a computer, a person can experience them again later.

与此同时，美国国家安全局运算编程速度达每秒200亿比特的计算机，能够通过其远程监控系统（RMS）“看到并听到”士兵在战场上的经历。当一枚微芯片被植入眼部视神经时——如今这种芯片直径仅30微米，而人的头发直径为50微米——它会以神经冲动的形式，从大脑中提取人物、经历、气味、景象和声音。将这些信息传输到计算机后，人日后还能再次体验它们。

The RMS system can send electromagnetic messages as signals through the nervous system to implanted individuals, affecting human performance. Microwaves can be used in such a way that a healthy person sees hallucinations or hears voices.

RMS系统可以通过神经系统，以信号形式向植入芯片者发送电磁信息，从而影响人的行为能力。微波可以被用来使健康人产生幻觉或听到声音。

Every thought, reaction, auditory perception and visual perception produces a particular kind of potential in the brain—spikes and patterns—and the EMF, or electromagnetic field, can be decoded into thoughts, images and sounds. EMF brain stimulation can change a person’s brain waves and affect, among other things, motor functions, causing painful muscle cramps and other effects.

每一个思想、反应、听觉和视觉感知都会在大脑中产生特定类型的电位，即尖峰和模式；电磁场（EMF）则可以被解码为思想、图像和声音。EMF大脑刺激能够改变人的脑电波，并影响运动机能等，造成痛苦的肌肉痉挛等后果。

The NSA’s electronic surveillance system can monitor and process millions of people simultaneously. Everyone has a unique bioelectrical resonance frequency in the brain, just as we each have a unique fingerprint system. EMF brain stimulation can be used to send desired, fully coded, pulsed electromagnetic signals into the brain, producing the desired auditory and visual effects in the target person. This is used, among other things, in electronic warfare.

美国国家安全局的电子监控系统可以同时监测并处理数百万人的信息。每个人的大脑都有独特的生物电共振频率，正如每个人都有独特的指纹一样。通过EMF大脑刺激，可以向大脑发送所需的、完全编码的脉冲电磁信号，使目标人物产生预期的听觉和视觉效果。这项技术被用于电子战等领域。

Astronauts were implanted before being sent into space. In this way, all their bodily functions could be monitored on Earth, and all their thoughts and emotions were recorded around the clock.

宇航员在被送入太空之前会被植入芯片。这样，地面人员就能监测他们所有的身体机能，并全天候记录他们所有的思想和情绪。

The Washington Post reported in May 1995 that Prince William of England had been implanted at the age of twelve. Thus, after a remote search, when a radio wave of a specified frequency is directed at a person’s microchip implant, the implant emits a signal that travels via satellite to a computer terminal at police headquarters, where the movements of a kidnapped person can be monitored and the person located.

《华盛顿邮报》于1995年5月报道称，英格兰的威廉王子在12岁时被植入了芯片。这样，在远程搜索之后，当特定频率的无线电波射向一个人的微芯片植入物时，植入物就会发出信号，经卫星传送到警察总部的计算机终端，从而监测被绑架者的行动并确定其位置。

The press has not reported that a person’s privacy has been lost for the rest of their life. The person can be manipulated in any way whatsoever. By using different frequencies, a person’s emotional life can be altered. They can be made aggressive or lethargic; their sexuality can be influenced; their thought signals can be read; their sleep can be affected; and even their subconscious, with motives unknown to themselves, can be uncovered without their knowledge. The perfect cyber-soldier has been created! The technology has been in use in some NATO countries since the early 1980s, without the non-military academic community having heard anything about it in its professional publications.

媒体从未报道过，一个人的隐私会在其余生中彻底丧失。这个人可以被任意操纵。通过使用不同频率，可以改变其情感生活；可以使其变得具有攻击性或昏昏欲睡；可以影响其性欲；可以读取其思想信号；可以影响其睡眠；甚至可以在其不知情的情况下，揭示连其本人都不了解的潜意识及其动机。完美的赛博士兵就此诞生！自20世纪80年代初以来，一些北约国家就一直在使用这项技术，而非军事学术界从未在其专业出版物中听说过此事。

The NSA’s Signals Intelligence can remotely monitor information from the human brain by decoding evoked potentials of 3–50 Hz and five-milliwatt electronic transmissions. In 1998, elderly people used as guinea pigs in both Gothenburg and Vienna were found to have matching brain injuries. Impaired blood circulation and oxygen deprivation in the right frontal lobe occur precisely in the area where brain implants are usually inserted. One Finnish patient case demonstrates cerebral atrophy and episodes of unconsciousness caused by oxygen deprivation. The purpose of mind-control systems today is to place a targeted individual or group of people under such complete external control that they act against their own convictions and interests. A person can even be programmed to commit a murder that they subsequently cannot remember. There are already alarming examples of this in the USA. The technology is used for political purposes.

美国国家安全局的信号情报部门能够通过解码3—50赫兹的诱发电位和5毫瓦的电子传输，远程监测人脑信息。1998年，哥德堡和维也纳两地被用作实验对象的老年人，均被发现具有相同的脑损伤。右额叶血液循环障碍和缺氧，恰好发生在通常植入大脑植入物的区域。芬兰的一名患者病例表现出脑萎缩以及由缺氧引起的意识丧失发作。如今，精神控制系统的目的是使目标个人或群体受到彻底的外部控制，以至于违背自己的信念和利益行事。一个人甚至可以被编程去实施谋杀，事后却对此毫无记忆。美国已经出现了令人不安的相关案例。这项技术也被用于政治目的。

Since 1980, the ESB system—electronic stimulation of the brain—has been misused, among other things, to control certain people selected as targets for one reason or another, without their knowledge. All international human-rights conventions prohibit such activity even in prisons, let alone among the civilian population. Most recently, on 22 January 1997, at John Glenn’s proposal, the US Senate addressed the problem of irradiating the civilian population, which includes directing electromagnetic radiation at people’s brain activity. In addition to electrode-based mind-control technology, chemical methods are also in use. “Silent war” also encompasses the NSA’s “kit” technologies used by the military and intelligence services, in which “mind-altering drugs”—various gaseous substances that have a paralysing effect on brain activity—are sprayed into ventilation ducts or water-supply networks. Bacteria and viruses have also been used for this purpose for intelligence purposes in various countries.

自1980年以来，ESB系统，即“大脑电子刺激”系统，一直被滥用，其用途之一就是在当事人不知情的情况下，控制某些因各种原因被选为目标的人。所有国际人权公约都禁止此类行为，即使在监狱中也不例外，更不用说针对平民了。美国参议院最近于1997年1月22日根据约翰·格伦的提议，讨论了对平民进行辐射的问题，其中包括将电磁辐射指向人的大脑活动。除了电极精神控制技术外，化学方法也在使用。“无声战争”还包括军方和情报机构使用的美国国家安全局“工具包”技术，其中将“改变精神的药物”——各种能够麻痹大脑活动的气态物质——喷洒到通风管道或供水管网中。各国还曾出于情报目的，将细菌和病毒用于这一用途。

Today’s cutting-edge technology—the connection of our brain activity, through microchips or, according to the latest advances, even without them, via satellites to supercomputers in the USA and Israel—is the greatest threat to humanity and privacy. The latest supercomputers are capable of processing the population of the entire globe.

当今的尖端技术，是通过微芯片，或者根据最新进展甚至无需微芯片，将人的大脑活动经卫星连接到美国和以色列的超级计算机。这是对人类和隐私最大的威胁。最新的超级计算机能够处理全球所有人口的信息。

The threat is the greatest in human history. Once everyone has been induced to accept a microchip into their body on false grounds, or even through compulsory legislation, as planned in the USA, the robotisation of humanity and the total disappearance of private life, extending to the control of thoughts, will become a reality. How many of us would want to hand over our entire lives, including our innermost thoughts, to an external controller who certainly does not have our best interests in mind? A totalitarian “new world order,” with its neurological communication systems, already exists to prevent people’s independent thinking and social and political activity.

这是人类历史上最大的威胁。一旦所有人都因虚假理由而被诱导在体内植入微芯片，甚至如美国所计划的那样通过强制性立法实施，人类的机器人化以及私人生活的彻底消失，包括思想受到控制，都将成为现实。我们中有多少人愿意把自己的一生，包括最隐秘的思想，交给一个肯定不会顾及我们利益的外部控制者？一个极权主义的“新世界秩序”及其神经通信系统已经存在，其目的就是阻止人们独立思考以及开展社会和政治活动。

Once our brain activity has been connected to supercomputers through radio implants and microchips, awakening will come too late. The threat can only be countered by becoming aware of the matter now and by familiarising ourselves with the literature in the field and the information obtained at international congresses.

一旦我们的大脑活动通过无线电植入物和微芯片连接到超级计算机，再醒悟就为时已晚。只有现在认识到这一问题，并了解该领域的文献以及国际大会上获得的信息，才能抵御这一威胁。

An interesting detail regarding this technology is that its concealment has been accomplished exceptionally well, among other things through the development, in eighteen languages, of the DSM manual of psychiatric disease classification by psychiatrists of the US intelligence service as a “cover-up” operation. It classifies all the methods used by intelligence services even today as symptoms of paranoid schizophrenia. Victims of mind-control experiments are therefore systematically diagnosed as mentally ill according to the symptoms we learned during our studies. We have never been taught that patients may be telling the truth after having been made, against their will, guinea pigs in military electronic, chemical and bacteriological warfare.

关于这项技术，一个值得注意的细节是，对它的隐瞒做得极为成功，其中包括美国情报机构的精神科医生将精神疾病分类DSM手册的开发作为一场“掩盖”行动，并以18种语言推行。该手册把情报机构至今仍在使用的所有方法都归类为偏执型精神分裂症的症状。因此，精神控制实验的受害者会按照我们在学习期间学到的症状，被系统性地诊断为精神病人。从来没有人教导我们，患者可能在被迫成为军事电子战、化学战和细菌战实验对象之后，讲述的其实是真相。

Our time is running out if we want to change the direction of military-medical research, not to mention the future of humanity.

如果我们想改变军事医学研究的方向，时间已经所剩无几，更不必说改变人类的未来了。
