claude双语译文（Bilingual Translation）

Mikrosiruimplantit, mind control ja kybernetiikka

Microchip Implants, Mind Control and Cybernetics

微芯片植入、心灵控制与控制论

Rauni-Leena Luukkanen-Kilde, specialist physician, Norway

劳尼-莱娜·卢卡宁-基尔德，专科医师，挪威

In 1948 Norbert Weiner published the book Cybernetics, by which was meant a complete neurological theory of control and communication, which was already in use at that time within a small circle. Yoneji Masuda, the father of the concept of the information society, expressed in 1980 his concern that our freedom is being threatened, in the manner of an Orwellian society, by this technology, which is wholly unknown to a large part of people. It connects people by means of microchips to a brain–satellite–computer link.

1948年，诺伯特·维纳（原文拼作 Weiner）出版了《控制论》（Cybernetics）一书，该书所指的是一种完整的神经学控制与通信理论，当时已在一个小圈子内投入使用。信息社会概念之父增田米二（Yoneji Masuda）于1980年表达了他的担忧：我们的自由正以奥威尔式社会的方式受到这项技术的威胁，而这项技术对很大一部分人来说是完全陌生的。它借助微芯片，把人连接到"大脑—卫星—计算机"的链路上。

The first brain implants were surgically placed as early as 1874 in Ohio, USA, and at the Karolinska Institutet brain electrodes were placed in children as early as 1946, without the parents’ knowledge.

最早的脑植入物早在1874年就在美国俄亥俄州通过外科手术植入；而在卡罗林斯卡学院（Karolinska Institutet），早在1946年就已把脑电极植入儿童体内，且家长并不知情。

In the 1950s and 1960s, especially in the USA, actual microchip implants began to be placed in animals and humans for research purposes, with the aim of studying, among other things, human behaviour, bodily functions and the brain. The so-called mind control methods aimed at manipulating human behaviour and senses. Influencing brain function became an important military weapon, especially for intelligence services.

20世纪50至60年代，尤其在美国，人们开始出于研究目的，在动物和人体内植入真正的微芯片植入物，目的包括研究人的行为、身体机能和大脑等。所谓的"心灵控制"方法旨在操纵人的行为和感官。影响大脑功能成为一种重要的军事武器，尤其对情报机构而言。

Thirty years ago, implants placed in the brain showed up on X-rays at a size of about one centimetre. Later the implants shrank to the size of a grain of rice and were made of silicone and later of gallium arsenide. Nowadays they have become small enough to be injected into the neck, the back, under the skin, or into various parts of the body in connection with operations. It is now almost impossible to remove them. The intention is indeed that in the future a microchip would be injected into every newborn, which would then serve, among other things, as identification for the whole rest of their life. In the USA these plans are already far advanced legislatively.

三十年前，植入大脑的植入物在X光片上显示的大小约为一厘米。后来植入物缩小到米粒大小，先用硅酮制成，后来用砷化镓制成。如今它们已经小到可以注射到颈部、背部、皮下，或在手术时植入身体的各个部位。现在几乎不可能再把它们取出。其意图也正是在将来给每一个新生儿注射一枚微芯片，它将作为身份识别等用途，伴随其整个余生。在美国，这些计划在立法层面上已经相当深入。

The same applies to Sweden, where Olof Palme gave permission in 1973 to implant, for example, prisoners, and where, according to the director general of Datainspektion, Jan Freesen, among others patients of old people’s homes were implanted in the mid-1980s. The Swedish state report SOU 1972:47 revealed the technology. These guinea pigs of human experiments can then be tracked anywhere, and their brain function can be influenced by supercomputers through remote control by changing frequencies.

瑞典同样如此：奥洛夫·帕尔梅于1973年批准例如对囚犯进行植入；而据数据监察局（Datainspektion）总局长扬·弗里森（Jan Freesen）称，20世纪80年代中期，养老院的病人等也被植入。瑞典国家报告 SOU 1972:47 披露了这项技术。这些人体实验的试验品随后可以在任何地方被追踪，其脑功能可以由超级计算机通过远程控制、改变频率来施加影响。

Prisoners, conscripts, patients of mental hospitals, the deaf, the blind, the mentally deficient, homosexuals, lonely women and other groups of people are used as guinea pigs. For example, the experiences of the inmates of a prison in the American state of Utah that have come to public attention are shocking.

囚犯、应征入伍者、精神病院的病人、聋人、盲人、智力障碍者、同性恋者、孤独的女性以及其他人群被用作试验品。例如，美国犹他州一所监狱的囚犯们已经公之于众的经历令人震惊。

The microchip operates by means of a low-frequency radio wave directed at it. By means of satellites, a person who has an implant can be located precisely. This technique was used, among other occasions, during the Iraq war, as Dr Carl Sanders told. He invented the IMI, the intelligence manned interface biochip, which is injected into people. During the Vietnam war soldiers were injected with the so-called Rambo chip, which increased the secretion of adrenaline.

微芯片借助对准它的低频无线电波运作。借助卫星，可以精确定位带有植入物的人。据卡尔·桑德斯（Carl Sanders）博士所述，这项技术曾用于伊拉克战争等场合。他发明了IMI（intelligence manned interface，“情报人员接口”）生物芯片，这种芯片被注射到人体内。越南战争期间，士兵们被注射了所谓的"兰博芯片"（Rambo chip），它会增加肾上腺素的分泌。

At the same time, the computers of the National Security Agency, programmed for 20 billion bits per second, are able, with their remote monitoring system, RMS, to “see and hear” what soldiers experienced on the battlefield. When a microchip, which today is only 30 micrometres in diameter (the diameter of a hair is 50 micrometres), has been placed in the optic nerve of the eye, it draws from the brain, as neural impulses, people, experiences, smells, visions and sounds. Once transferred to a computer, the person can experience them again later.

与此同时，美国国家安全局（National Security Agency）以每秒200亿比特的规格编程的计算机，能够凭借其远程监控系统RMS"看到并听到"士兵们在战场上经历的一切。当一枚如今直径仅30微米（头发的直径为50微米）的微芯片被置于眼睛的视神经之中时，它会从大脑中以神经冲动的形式吸取人物、经历、气味、影像和声音。传输到计算机之后，当事人以后可以再次体验这些内容。

With the RMS system, electromagnetic messages are sent as signals through the nervous system to implanted individuals, which affects the person’s performance capacity. With microwaves, healthy people can be caused to have hallucinations and to hear voices.

通过RMS系统，电磁信息以信号的形式经由神经系统发送给被植入的个体，从而影响人的工作能力（suorituskyky，即行为表现能力）。利用微波，可以使健康人产生幻觉并听到声音。

Every thought, reaction, hearing and visual perception causes in the brain a certain kind of potential (spikes, pattern), and the EMF (electromagnetic field) can be decoded into thoughts, pictures and sounds. EMF brain stimulation can change a person’s brain waves and affect, among other things, muscle motor function, causing painful muscle cramps and so on.

每一个想法、反应、听觉和视觉感知都会在大脑中引起一种特定的电位（尖峰、模式），而EMF（电磁场）可以被解码为想法、图像和声音。EMF脑刺激可以改变人的脑电波，并影响例如肌肉运动功能，引起疼痛的肌肉痉挛等。

The NSA’s electronic tracking system can simultaneously track and process millions of people. Everyone has their own unique bioelectric resonance frequency in the brain, just as we have our own unique fingerprint system. With EMF brain stimulation, the desired fully coded, pulsating electromagnetic signals can be sent into the brain, causing the desired sound and visual effects in target persons. This is used, among other things, in electronic warfare.

NSA的电子追踪系统可以同时追踪并处理数百万人。每个人的大脑中都有自己独一无二的生物电共振频率，正如我们也各有独一无二的指纹系统一样。借助EMF脑刺激，可以向大脑发送所需的、完全编码的脉冲电磁信号，使目标人物产生所需的声音和视觉效果。这被用于例如电子战。

Astronauts were implanted before being sent into space. In this way all their bodily functions could be tracked from the Earth, and all their thoughts and emotions were registered around the clock.

宇航员在被送入太空之前接受了植入。这样，他们的全部身体机能都可以从地球上被追踪，他们的所有想法和情绪也被全天候记录下来。

The Washington Post reported in May 1995 that Prince William of England was implanted at the age of 12. Thus, when a radio wave of a specified frequency is directed, after a remote query, at a person’s microchip implant, a signal is sent from it, which travels via satellites to a computer screen at police headquarters, from where the movements of a kidnapped person can be followed and the person located.

《华盛顿邮报》1995年5月报道，英国的威廉王子在12岁时被植入。这样，在远程查询之后，当以特定频率的无线电波对准某人的微芯片植入物时，会从中发出信号，信号经由卫星传送到警察总部的计算机屏幕上，从那里可以追踪被绑架者的行动并确定其位置。

The press has not reported that privacy is lost for the rest of a person’s life. The person can be manipulated in any way. By using different frequencies, a person’s emotional life can be made to change. They can be made aggressive, lethargic; their sexuality can be influenced, their thought signals can be read, their dreams can be influenced, and even their own unknown subconscious with its motives can be found out without their knowing. The perfect cyber-soldier has been created! The technology has been in use in some NATO countries since the early 1980s, without the non-military academic population having heard anything about it in their professional publications.

媒体并未报道，一个人的隐私从此终身丧失。此人可以被随意操纵。通过使用不同的频率，可以使人的情感生活发生改变。可以使其变得具有攻击性、昏昏沉沉；可以影响其性方面（sexuality），可以读取其思维信号，可以影响其梦，甚至可以在其不知情的情况下查明其自身都不了解的潜意识及其动机。完美的赛博士兵已经被造出来了！这项技术自20世纪80年代初起就已在一些北约国家使用，而非军事领域的学术人群在自己的专业出版物中对此却一无所闻。

The NSA’s Signals Intelligence is able to remotely monitor information from the human brain by decoding the evoked potentials, 3–50 Hz, 5 millivolt electronic transmissions. In elderly people used as guinea pigs, identical brain damage has been found in both Gothenburg and Vienna. Disturbance of blood circulation and oxygen deficiency in the right frontal lobe occur precisely in the area where brain implants are usually operated. One Finnish patient case shows brain atrophy and attacks of loss of consciousness due to oxygen deficiency. The purpose of mind control systems today is to bring the target person or group of people under such complete external control that they act against their own convictions and interests.

NSA的信号情报部门（Signals Intelligence）能够通过解码诱发（evoked）电位——3–50赫兹、5毫伏的电子发射——来远程监测来自人脑的信息。在被用作试验品的老年人身上，哥德堡和维也纳都发现了相同的脑损伤。右额叶的血液循环障碍和缺氧，恰恰出现在通常实施脑植入手术的区域。一例芬兰患者病例显示出脑萎缩，以及由缺氧引起的昏迷发作。如今心灵控制系统的目的，是使作为目标的个人或人群处于如此彻底的外部控制之下，以至于其行为违背自己的信念和利益。

They can be programmed, for example, to commit a murder, of which they afterwards remember nothing. There are already alarming examples of this in the USA. The technology is used for political purposes.

例如，可以把此人编程去实施一桩谋杀，而事后对此毫无记忆。美国已经有这方面令人担忧的例子。这项技术被用于政治目的。

Since 1980 the ESB system (electronic stimulation of the brain) has been misused, among other things, to control certain people chosen as targets for one reason or another without their knowledge. All international human rights conventions prohibit such activity, even in prisons, not to mention the civilian population. At the proposal of John Glenn, the US Senate last dealt with the problem of irradiating the civilian population on 22 January 1997, to which the directing of electromagnetic radiation at people’s brain function belongs. In addition to electrode-based mind control technology, chemical methods are also in use. The “silent war” also covers NSA “kit” techniques used by military and intelligence services, whereby “mind altering drugs”, various gaseous substances that paralyse brain function, are sprayed into ventilation ducts or the water supply network. Bacteria and viruses have also demonstrably been used for this purpose in various countries.

自1980年起，ESB系统（electronic stimulation of the brain，脑电刺激）已被滥用，例如在当事人不知情的情况下，控制某些出于这样或那样的原因被选作目标的人。所有国际人权公约都禁止此类活动，即使在监狱里也是如此，更不用说针对平民了。应约翰·格伦（John Glenn）的提议，美国参议院最近一次处理平民遭受辐射的问题是在1997年1月22日，把电磁辐射对准人的脑功能也属于这一问题。除基于电极的心灵控制技术之外，也在使用化学方法。“无声战争”（silent war）还包括军方和情报机构使用的NSA"套件"（kit）技术，即把"改变心智的药物"（mind altering drugs）——各种使脑功能麻痹的气态物质——喷入通风管道或供水管网。细菌和病毒也已在各国被用于此目的，且有据可查。

Today’s high technology — the connecting of our brain function, by means of microchips or even without them, according to the latest achievements, via satellites to supercomputers in the USA and Israel — is the greatest threat to humanity and privacy. The latest supercomputers are able to process the entire population of the globe.

当今的高科技——借助微芯片，或者按最新成果甚至无需微芯片，通过卫星把我们的大脑功能连接到美国和以色列的超级计算机——是对人性和隐私的最大威胁。最新的超级计算机能够处理全球全部人口。

The threat is the greatest in the history of mankind. As soon as everyone has been lured into taking a microchip into their body on false grounds, or even through compulsory legislation, as has been planned in the USA, the robotisation of mankind and the control of private life and thoughts will have been realised. How many of us would want to hand over our whole life, even our most secret thoughts, to an outside controller who certainly does not have our interests in mind? A totalitarian “new world order” with its neurological communication systems already exists in order to prevent people’s independent thinking and social and political activity.

这一威胁是人类历史上最大的。一旦所有人都在错误的理由之下被诱使把微芯片放进自己体内，甚至通过强制性立法（正如美国所计划的那样），人类的机器人化以及对私人生活和思想的控制就将成为现实。我们当中有多少人愿意把自己的整个生活，连最隐秘的想法都包括在内，交给一个肯定心里没有我们利益的外部控制者？一个带有神经学通信系统的极权主义"新世界秩序"已经存在，其目的是阻止人们的独立思考以及社会和政治活动。

Once our brain function has been connected to supercomputers by means of radio implants and microchips, waking up is too late. The threat can be averted only by becoming aware of the matter now, and by familiarising oneself with the literature of the field and the information obtained at international congresses.

一旦我们的大脑功能借助无线电植入物和微芯片被连接到超级计算机，再醒悟就太晚了。只有现在就认清这件事，并了解该领域的文献以及国际会议上获得的信息，才能消除这一威胁。

An interesting detail about this technology is that it has been noted that its concealment has succeeded excellently, for example through the development of the psychiatric diagnostic classification manual DSM into 18 languages by psychiatrists of US intelligence services as a “cover up” operation. In it, all the methods that intelligence services use even today are classified as symptoms of paranoid schizophrenia. Victims of mind control experiments are thus systematically diagnosed as mentally ill according to the symptoms we learned already during our studies. We have never been taught that patients may be telling the truth after having been made, against their will, guinea pigs of military electronic, chemical and bacteriological warfare.

关于这项技术，一个有趣的细节是，据确认，对它的隐瞒已经极为成功，例如，美国情报机构的精神科医生为了"掩盖"（cover up）行动，把精神科疾病分类手册DSM发展为18种语言的版本。该手册把情报机构至今仍在使用的所有手段都归类为偏执型精神分裂症的症状。因此，心灵控制实验的受害者会按照我们在求学期间就学到的症状，被系统性地诊断为精神病患者。从来没有人教过我们：患者在违背自己意愿成为军事性的电子、化学和细菌战的试验品之后，可能说的是真话。

Our time is running out, if we want to change the direction of military-medical research, not to mention the future of humankind.

如果我们想改变军事医学研究的方向，更不用说人类的未来，我们的时间正在耗尽。
